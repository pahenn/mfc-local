1 · The number we compare

Driven's Net Sales = gross − discounts and excludes tax. The MFC figure that matches it is the pre-tax gross invoice (invoiceTotalAmount − tax) — not the post-prepayment balance (invoiceTotalAmount − prepaid), which reads $0 for prepaid fleets even though the store rang real sales. Below: share of fleet/store/weeks reconciling within 5% under each candidate basis.

Moving from the balance to the pre-tax gross invoice lifts the within-5% match rate to ~99% of rows with both sides present, and collapses the >5% dollar variance dramatically (next chart).

5 · Driven sales MFC never invoiced

These are stores Driven reported but where SoundBilling has no invoice for that (fleet, store, week) — $4,301,636 of Driven net sales. It's not a billing-amount problem: it's overwhelmingly national fleet-management, leasing and rental accounts (Enterprise, Element, LeasePlan, Hertz, ARI/Holman, Emkay, Merchants…) that are billed centrally and never flow through per-store SoundBilling invoices. A small slice ($76,339) is education / prepaid programs. The local standard rows are the only ones worth chasing as possible intake or store-key gaps.
